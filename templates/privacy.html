Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This kindergarten portal processes child, parent, guardian, staff, attendance, contact, and government identification information for childcare and school-safety purposes.

Information we collect

We collect information submitted through the portal, including names, dates of birth, contact details, classroom records, attendance records, guardian authorizations, identification records, photos, care notes, and message delivery logs.

How information is used

Information is used to manage enrollment, authorized pickups, attendance, emergency notifications, daily care records, staff access, and required operational records. Access is limited to authorized staff.

Sharing and providers

Information may be processed by hosting, database, SMS, and email providers only where required to operate the service. We do not sell personal information or use advertising trackers.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained only as long as required for childcare operations, legal obligations, safety records, and approved school retention periods. Parents or guardians may request access, correction, export, or deletion subject to applicable legal requirements.

Your choices and rights

Contact the school administrator to review, correct, export, or delete personal information. You may also request withdrawal of optional communications where withdrawal does not affect required childcare safety information.

Security

Role-based access, encrypted HTTPS connections, protected sessions, and audit records are used to reduce unauthorized access and misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely risk-free.
